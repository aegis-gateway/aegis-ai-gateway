# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful experience for everyone — regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences
- Give and receive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Dismissing or belittling contributions
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **hello@aegisgateway.ai**.
All reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and to ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.
